Case file: Chobani
  • Product: thick Greek-style yogurt with about twice the protein of regular yogurt and no preservatives. The recipe was tested and improved for more than a year before launch.
  • Target audience: not only women (the usual target) — also men and people who did not like yogurt before.
  • Place: the normal dairy aisle of big supermarkets, not expensive specialty stores or the gourmet section.
  • Price point: about $1 a cup — normal yogurt cost about 65 cents, premium Greek yogurt $1.34.
  • Promotion: mostly word of mouth, social media and talking directly to customers.
  • Result: Greek yogurt grew from under 1% of the US yogurt market (2006) to about half of it (2014).

Step 1. Fix the bad questions. They are leading, hypothetical or about features. Rewrite them, then compare.

  1. ✗ Would you buy a mango-flavoured kefir?
    ✓ Tell me about the last time you bought kefir. Which one did you choose and why?
  2. ✗ Do you like our new pack design?
    ✓ What do you look at first when you choose yoghurt on the shelf?
  3. ✗ Which new flavours do you want?
    ✓ Which flavours have you tried recently? What didn’t you like about them?
  4. ✗ You’d pay 10 hryvnias more for organic milk, wouldn’t you?
    ✓ How much do you usually pay for milk? When do you choose a more expensive one? (Careful: a question tag can bias the answer!)
