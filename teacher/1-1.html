Cheat sheet: how to talk about market research for cheeseШпаргалка: як говорити про дослідження ринку сиру

The main idea: a new cheese, format, pack or price should be based on data about shoppers, retail chains and chefs — not on the opinion of the boss. In the cheese business, research means tastings and sensory panels, retail sales data, store checks, complaints, talking to category managers and chefs, and price tests.Головна думка: новий сир, формат, упаковка чи ціна мають спиратися на дані про покупців, мережі й шеф-кухарів — а не на думку керівника. У сирному бізнесі дослідження — це дегустації та сенсорні панелі, дані продажів мереж, перевірки в магазинах, скарги, розмови з категорійними менеджерами й шефами, цінові тести.

Answers and model answersВідповіді та зразки

10A — model (Parmigiano Reggiano vs Grana Padano)

Both brands attract customers with Italian tradition; their target audience is people who cook Italian food. Parmigiano promises a long maturation and a protected origin, so customer satisfaction comes from the strong, nutty taste. Grana Padano is a formidable competitor: it has a lot of shelf space and a lower price point. For the ideal customer the pain point is price — real parmesan is expensive, so many choose the cheaper option.

10B — better questions

  1. Tell me about the last time you bought hard cheese. Which one did you choose and why?
  2. What do you look at first when you choose cheese on the shelf?
  3. Which cheeses have you tried recently? What didn’t you like about them?
  4. How much do you usually pay for hard cheese? When do you choose a more expensive one? (the question tag in the original biases the answer)

Good survey (12-month aged cheese, pre-grated): 1 How often do you buy hard cheese? (multiple-choice, quant.) · 2 Do you buy pre-grated cheese? (yes/no, quant.) · 3 How satisfied are you with the hard cheese you buy now, 1–10? (rating, quant.) · 4 Tell us about the last time you cooked with hard cheese. (open-ended, qual.) · 5 Where do you usually buy cheese? (multiple-choice) · 6 What don’t you like about the cheese you buy now? (open-ended, qual.)

10D — model script

Our target audience is people who cook Italian food at home and restaurants that can’t afford imported parmesan. Over half of the respondents admitted that they buy cheaper cheese because of the price, and several raised concerns that Ukrainian hard cheese melts badly. The burning problem is an affordable cheese that tastes and melts like parmesan, so it warrants a new product. As a next step, we’ll run a blind tasting with 100 shoppers and a test with five restaurants to get hard data.

★ Challenges — model replies

  • Hard data or compliments? — Not compliments: 60 of 100 shoppers chose our sample in the blind tasting.
  • Fifteen people isn’t a sample size. — Right, that was a pilot. Next we’ll test with 100 shoppers in three cities.
